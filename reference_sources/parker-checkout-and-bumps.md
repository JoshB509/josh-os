# Parker's checkout and bump copy

Reference copy, not CareerOS material. Parker Worth's checkout pages for three of his products (Big Bang Launch Plan, Digital Storytelling Masterclass 1.0, Email 365) and three order bump examples, each with the fill-in template he built from it. The original is `Parker-Checkout-Bumps-and-Templates.docx` in this folder. Added 6 Oct 2026.

## What to take for CareerOS checkouts

- Name every product and attach a personal result to it ("the swipe file that helped me generate multiple 5-figure launches").
- Give a true reason to decide now. His bumps say "one-time offer, only on this page". Ours can only say the price is only here, because the in-app upgrade sells the same things later.
- Say how much is in the box (50 prompts, 265 subject lines).
- Curiosity bullets that name a moment and hold back the method, with the objection answered in a bracket at the end.
- First-person buttons with the name and the price ("Yes! Add the Broadcast Bible for $27").

## What to leave

- The register. Kardashians, exploding piggy banks and "cash in your bank account every time you wake up" read as hype to senior buyers.
- The layout. His Kajabi checkout runs 13 bullets in a single column beside the card form, and the bump is a grey box with a giant underlined "ONE TIME OFFER". Ours keeps the bullets to five or six and puts the extras behind a reveal.
- "Plus a lot more...", which tells the buyer nothing.
