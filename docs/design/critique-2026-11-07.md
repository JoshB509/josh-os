# Josh OS design critique, 7 November 2026

This critique uses the Owl-Listener/designer-skills `visual-critique` skills: visual hierarchy, information density, typography, colour, affordance, composition and brand consistency. Each dimension follows the skills' format with an observation, the problem, the fix, and a rating of `pass`, `minor issue` or `major issue`.

It covers the redesign work in progress (commit `1d67abf`) running with demo data: 6 videos across the stages, 5 carousels and 5 emails. The screens reviewed are Today, YouTube videos, Editing, Email, Team, a Team profile and a video page, each at desktop (1440×900) and phone (390×844) size. Font sizes, weights and contrast were measured in the browser rather than judged by eye.

## Summary

The new structure is right. Each agent appears where their work lives and says what needs Josh in plain language, which fixes the "config page" problem from the earlier Team page. The main gap is that the new components have no styling yet, so the screens read as flat, unordered text. Underneath that sit five older system problems:

1. **No container for the agents.** The desk, its items and its quick replies run into the page with no boundary, so Sam's message blends into the YouTube board below it (common region, hierarchy).
2. **Faint grey text fails contrast.** `--faint` (#A3A6AD) measures 2.44:1 on white against the 4.5:1 minimum, and it appears 18 times on the YouTube page alone, on dates, counts and empty-column labels (colour).
3. **Too many type sizes.** Screens use 7 sizes: 11.67, 12, 13, 14, 15, 24 and 32px. The 11.67px size comes from `font-size:12px` scaled down, and the 13px and 15px sizes sit off any scale (typography).
4. **Phones get a desktop layout squeezed down.** Desk items overflow sideways, 15 controls on the YouTube page are shorter than 32px, and the bottom tab bar has no styling, so it leaks onto desktop as stray buttons (responsive, Fitts's law).
5. **Old banners compete with the agents.** The "October focus" banner is out of date and sits at the same weight as Sam's briefing, and the "Demo workspace" banner sits above everything (hierarchy).

## Visual hierarchy (`critique-visual-hierarchy`)

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Entry point | major issue |
| Eye flow | major issue |
| Weight | minor issue |
| Emphasis | major issue |

**Entry point.**
- *Observation:* On YouTube, Editing and Email the eye lands first on the 32px page title, then on a small 36px avatar with the name and role run together ("SamContent & YouTube Manager").
- *Problem:* The briefing is the most important thing on the page, but it has the lowest weight of anything near the top. The name and role have no space between them because the styles that separate them don't exist yet.
- *Fix:* Put the desk in a card with the agent's colour as a left accent. Show a 48px portrait, the name at subheading size and the role as a caption on its own line, and give the message body size with full text colour so it becomes the entry point after the title.

**Eye flow.**
- *Observation:* The order on YouTube runs title, desk, "October focus" banner, stage chips, Board/List toggle, then the board.
- *Problem:* Three separate horizontal bands sit between Sam's briefing and the board, and the eye has to cross the stale October banner to reach the work.
- *Fix:* Remove the planning banner from the board, since Sam's briefing replaces it. The order becomes title, desk, then the board controls.

**Weight.**
- *Observation:* Every desk item is a bordered button with a bold title. Five of them in a row carry the same weight as the page's primary "New video" button.
- *Problem:* Item titles are bold (700) while page section titles are 600, so the list outweighs the headings above it.
- *Fix:* Show items as rows in one list with dividers, the title at 500 weight and the sub-line as a caption. Keep 600 and above for headings only.

**Emphasis.**
- *Observation:* On Today, all four people get identical treatment, and items that need Josh's decision ("Review …", "Watch …") look the same as plain status lines.
- *Problem:* Nothing tells Josh which item to deal with first.
- *Fix:* Give "needs your eye" items a gold dot and sort them first. Count them in the desk header ("2 for you"), and give the status-only items quieter text.

## Information density (`critique-information-density`)

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Cognitive load | minor issue |
| Content priority | major issue |
| Scanning pattern | major issue |
| Progressive disclosure | pass |

- **Cognitive load.** *Observation:* Sam's desk on the YouTube page lists carousel items. *Problem:* The YouTube page is about videos, so carousel items there split Josh's attention between two jobs. *Fix:* Filter desk items by page, so Sam's desk shows video items on YouTube and carousel items on Carousels, while Today shows everything.
- **Content priority.** *Observation:* On phones the Today greeting, date and subtitle take up 30% of the first screen before any agent appears. *Problem:* The team's briefing falls below the fold. *Fix:* Put the date and greeting on one line, drop the subtitle on phones, and start the first desk above the fold.
- **Scanning pattern.** *Observation:* Desk items wrap as inline chips of different widths. *Problem:* The left edges don't line up, so the eye can't scan down a column. *Fix:* Use a single left-aligned list with icons in a fixed 20px column.
- **Progressive disclosure.** *Observation:* The profile settings now sit inside "How Sam works", and the desk shows four items plus "and 3 more". *Pass:* This is the right pattern. "and 3 more" should become a link to the full list on the agent's page.

## Typography (`critique-typography`)

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Scale usage | major issue |
| Readability | minor issue |
| Consistency | minor issue |
| Token compliance | major issue |

- **Scale.** *Observation:* The measured sizes were 11.67, 12, 13, 14, 15, 24 and 32px. *Problem:* Three of those steps are less than 1.1× apart, which is scale drift. *Fix:* Use a six-step scale with `--fs-caption` 12px, `--fs-small` 13px, `--fs-body` 15px, `--fs-sub` 18px, `--fs-h2` 24px and `--fs-h1` 32px (36px on Today), and stop using 11.67px and 14px for body text.
- **Readability.** *Observation:* Body text is 14px with a 1.5 line height, and the Today briefing lines stretch to about 150 characters at 1440px. *Problem:* The skill's minimum body size for desktop is 16px, and a readable line runs 45 to 75 characters. *Fix:* Move body to 15px, keeping a dense app feel with Manrope's large x-height. Set desk messages to 16px with `max-width:62ch`.
- **Consistency.** *Observation:* Agent names show at 14px bold in desks, 24px in profile cards and 34px on the profile page. *Fix:* Use `--fs-sub` 600 for names in desks and cards and `--fs-h1` on the profile page.
- **Token compliance.** *Observation:* `team.css` uses raw sizes (12, 13, 14, 15, 22, 28, 34px) and `brand.css` sets `body{font-size:14px}`. *Fix:* Define the scale as tokens in `tokens.css` and refer to them everywhere in the new components.

## Colour (`critique-color`)

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Contrast | major issue |
| Palette coherence | minor issue |
| Semantic use | minor issue |
| Accessibility | minor issue |

- **Contrast.** *Observation:* `--faint` #A3A6AD on #FFFFFF measures 2.44:1, against 4.5:1 required, in 18 places on YouTube, 1 on Team and on placeholders. `--muted` #6E717A on `--callout` #F5F5F4 measures 4.47:1, also below 4.5:1. *Fix:* Change `--faint` to #6C6F77 (5.0:1 on white, 4.6:1 on #F5F5F4) and `--muted` to #5F626A (6.1:1 on white, 5.6:1 on #F5F5F4). In dark mode `--faint` #71747B measures 3.8:1 on #191919, so it changes to #8A8D94 (5.3:1).
- **Palette coherence.** *Observation:* The agent colours (#2383e2, #9065b0, #d9730d, #0f7b6c) come from the old Notion palette, while the brand is ink and gold. *Problem:* Four saturated colours next to gold weaken the brand. *Fix:* Use each agent's colour only as a thin accent (photo ring and the desk's left edge), never as a fill behind text.
- **Semantic use.** *Observation:* Stage dots rely on colour alone. *Fix:* Every stage dot already sits next to its stage name, so this is a pass for stages. The new "needs your eye" marker needs both a gold dot and the word "Review", "Watch" or "Pick".
- **Accessibility.** *Observation:* Focus rings use gold (#F5C21B), which measures 1.66:1 on white against the 3:1 that interactive outlines need. *Fix:* Draw focus as a 2px ink ring with a gold halo, so focus shows even in forced-colours mode.

## Affordance (`critique-affordance`)

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Clickability signals | minor issue |
| State visibility | minor issue |
| CTA clarity | major issue |
| Action discoverability | minor issue |

- **Clickability.** *Observation:* The desk items have borders and chevrons, so they look clickable, which is correct. The Team person cards don't, because they render as plain text rows. *Fix:* Make each person card a raised surface with a hover lift and a "Go to desk" link.
- **State visibility.** *Observation:* Loading the Team page shows only "Loading your team…". *Fix:* Show a skeleton desk with an avatar circle and two grey lines, following `loading-states`, so the layout doesn't shift when the data arrives.
- **CTA clarity.** *Observation:* Quick replies ("New carousel", "New video", "Open carousels") are outlined buttons at the same weight as the items, and the page header has its own "New video" button. *Problem:* On YouTube, two "New video" buttons compete. *Fix:* Show quick replies as small text chips under the message, and skip any that repeat the page's own primary action.
- **Discoverability.** *Observation:* "Hide" on the desk shows as plain text with no icon, and "and 3 more" can't be clicked. *Fix:* Use a "Minimise" chevron button, and make "and 3 more" a link that opens the full list.

## Composition (`critique-composition`)

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Balance | minor issue |
| Whitespace | major issue |
| Rhythm | major issue |
| Gestalt | major issue |

- **Balance.** *Observation:* On Today at 1440px, every desk spans the full width of the page, leaving the right half of each one empty. *Fix:* Show the desks in a two-column grid from 1100px up, with the week strip running full width below.
- **Whitespace.** *Observation:* Consecutive desks have no space between them (on Today, Tariq starts right under Sam's chips). *Fix:* Space sections at 32px and stack items inside a desk at 8px.
- **Rhythm.** *Observation:* The spacing values in use are 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 28 and 40px. *Fix:* Use a 4px-based spacing scale (`--sp-1` 4px through `--sp-8` 64px) for all new components.
- **Gestalt.** *Observation:* Proximity fails in the desk header, where the name, role and "Hide" button stack with no grouping, and in the Team list, where "Go to desk" floats between two people. Common region is missing everywhere, since no desk has a surface. *Fix:* Give each desk and each person card its own card surface. Inside a desk, group the header items in one row, the message and items in a body block, and the quick replies in a footer.

## Responsive and Fitts's law (`responsive-design`, `fitts-law`, `navigation-patterns`)

- *Observation:* On phones, desk items run off the right edge of the screen and the page scrolls sideways. The bottom tab bar isn't styled, and it shows up on desktop as loose buttons in the middle of the page. On the YouTube page, 15 controls are shorter than 32px.
- *Fix:* Hide the tab bar above 760px, and below that fix it to the bottom with 56px-tall tabs and safe-area padding. Make all desk items full width on phones, with tap targets at least 44px tall and text that wraps instead of overflowing.

## Re-critique after the fixes

The same skills ran again on the same screens and demo data, with the same measurements.

| Skill | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Visual hierarchy | 3 major, 1 minor | pass. The page title comes first, then the agent's desk with their message as the entry point, then the work |
| Information density | 2 major, 1 minor | pass. Sam's desk shows video items and talks about videos on YouTube, and carousel items on Carousels |
| Typography | 2 major, 2 minor | minor issue (see below) |
| Colour | 1 major, 3 minor | pass. No grey text fails contrast (the faint grey failed 18 times on YouTube before) |
| Affordance | 1 major, 3 minor | minor issue (see below) |
| Composition | 3 major, 1 minor | pass. Desks and people are cards on the spacing scale |
| Brand consistency | skipped | ready to run against the new `mood.md`, `voice.md` and `tokens.md` |

**Measured changes**
- Faint grey text failing contrast went from 18 instances to 0 on YouTube and from 1 to 0 on Team. The remaining items in the measurement log are semi-transparent hover tints that the script reads as solid colour, and they pass once composited over white.
- Type sizes in the new components now come only from the scale (12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 24, 32 and 36px), and 11.67px is gone. The 40px value on Team is the initial on Max's avatar.
- All new controls are at least 32px tall on desktop and 44 to 56px on phones, and the tab bar tabs are 56px.
- Sam's "for you" count matches across Today, Team and his desk because it uses one rule (`deskNeeds`), and Laia's message and badge now agree.

**What's left (older screens, next pass)**
- *Typography, minor issue:* the older YouTube board, Email list and video page still use 14px body text and their own sizes. Moving them onto the tokens is a page-by-page job.
- *Affordance, minor issue:* 14 controls on the YouTube board and 12 on Email are shorter than 32px, all from the older components (stage chips, Board and List toggle, filter tabs). These need resizing in the same pass.
- *Composition:* on Today at 1440px, Laia's desk sits under Sam's, leaving space under Tariq's. A masonry layout would fill it, but reading order matters more, so it stays.
- *Video page:* the redesign doesn't reach it yet. It still opens on the Overview tab with "Saved on this PC", and the script writer and thumbnail maker are unchanged, as agreed.

## Whole app

A second round covered every remaining screen at desktop and phone size: Calendar, Library, Archive, Carousels and the carousel editor, every tab of the video page, the email editor, and the separate Tasks, Lessons and Admin pages. It used the same visual critiques plus `navigation-patterns`, `jakobs-law`, `responsive-design` and `fitts-law`.

### Findings

| # | Finding | Skill | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tasks, Lessons and Admin each had their own older sidebar with no Today, Team, Carousels or Editing, and on phones Tasks used a sideways-scrolling menu instead of the tab bar | navigation-patterns, jakobs-law | major issue |
| 2 | The video page header stacked the stage, "Saved on this PC", "Copy video context", "Video folder" and the tabs with no clear next action, and the breadcrumb read "Today" when a video was opened from Today | visual hierarchy | major issue |
| 3 | "Archive project" in red and "Export project JSON" sat in the middle of the Overview tab | affordance | major issue |
| 4 | The Thumbnails tab had two primary buttons, "Open creator" for the experimental layered creator and "Import thumbnails" | affordance, CTA clarity | major issue |
| 5 | The script tab was headed "One story. Room to think." and showed "1 words" | typography, voice | minor issue |
| 6 | The calendar showed technical copy ("UTC+04:00", "Local planning", "Calendar email moves stay local"), and on phones the month grid fitted 4 of 7 days | density, responsive | major issue |
| 7 | Phone tab rows clipped ("Script", "Published"), and the Email page scrolled sideways on phones | responsive | major issue |
| 8 | On phones, 15 controls on Tasks, 9 on the video page and 12 on Email were under 40px (status dropdowns at 27px, filter tabs at 30px), and older stylesheets used 11px text | fitts-law, typography | minor issue |

### Fixes

1. `shell.js` builds one navigation list for every page, rebuilding the Tasks, Lessons and Admin sidebars with each page's own classes, and adds the phone tab bar and a "More" sheet listing every page. Admin's `data-section` links keep working.
2. `screens.js` puts a next-step line on every video, naming who is on it with a single action button (for example "Sam: The edit is ready for your review, and it still needs a thumbnail" with "Pick a thumbnail"). A video opened from Today or Team is filed under YouTube videos or Editing, so the breadcrumb is right. "Saved on this PC" now reads "Saved".
3. Archive and Export moved into a collapsed "More actions" at the bottom of the Overview tab.
4. The layered creator is now a quiet secondary strip, so "Import thumbnails" is the only primary button.
5. The script tab is headed "Script", the word count handles one word, and script text is 16px with a 72ch measure.
6. The calendar's technical copy is removed, and on phones the calendar shows a four-week agenda with the full month a turn of the phone away.
7. Tab rows scroll sideways on phones, and the Email filter and tools rows no longer overflow.
8. `system.css` loads on every page and puts body text, headings and control sizes on the tokens. It sets a 44px minimum for touch targets on phones, including dropdowns and expandable headers on the older pages, and moves every 11px size to the 12px caption.
