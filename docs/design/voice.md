# Voice

Every line in Josh OS is written as a smart colleague talking to Josh. Team members speak in the first person and Josh is "you".

**Tone:** direct, warm and brief. State the fact, then the next step.

**Rules:**
- Write at a grade 12 reading level, clear without sounding academic.
- No em dashes. Restructure the sentence instead.
- Don't split a sentence with a colon.
- Avoid "it isn't X, it's Y" and other constructions that say what something is not before saying what it is.
- Don't tell the reader how surprising something is or why it matters. Say the thing.
- Don't use the words quiet, quietly, land, landed, honest or honestly, and don't call a meeting "the room".
- Connect a consequence to its cause with a comma and a conjunction rather than a new sentence.
- Use full sentences in messages, never fragments like "Tired. Hungry. Cold."
- Name actual things. "Review “The update formula”" beats "1 item needs attention".

**Microcopy:**

| State | Example |
|---|---|
| Briefing | "4 carousels are due this week, and the first one is due tomorrow." |
| Needs Josh | "Review “The update formula”" · "Watch “Promotion conversations that work”" |
| Empty | "Nothing to edit right now. Once a video is marked Recorded, I'll pick it up." |
| Loading | "Getting the team's notes" |
| Saved | "Saved" |
| Error (system) | "That didn't save. Your changes are still here, so try again in a moment." |

**Button labels:** start with a verb and name the object, like "New carousel", "Go to Sam's desk" or "Add lesson".
