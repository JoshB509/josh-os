# Mood

Josh OS is Josh's private studio for running CareerOS. It should feel like a calm, well-run office where a small team of capable people already has things in hand and tells you what needs you.

**Personality:** confident, warm, organised and direct. It reads like a professional workspace with real people in it, closer to a good chief of staff than to a dashboard.

**Aesthetic:** a white canvas with near-neutral greys and ink (#15171C) for weight. CareerOS gold (#F5C21B) appears as the one accent, used sparingly for what needs attention. Outfit for headings, Manrope for everything else. Photos of the team bring the warmth, while the interface around them stays quiet.

**Do:**
- Put people first. The team members' photos and words lead each workspace.
- Keep one clear thing to do per screen.
- Use generous space, plain surfaces and soft shadows.
- Use gold only for "needs you" and for focus.

**Don't:**
- Use several bright colours side by side. The agent colours are thin accents only.
- Build dense admin tables as the first thing on a screen.
- Add decoration, gradients or illustrations where a photo or plain words do the job.
- Use playful animation. Motion stays short (150 to 250ms) and eases out.
