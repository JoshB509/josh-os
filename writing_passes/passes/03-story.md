# Story specialist

Use shared.md and only this pass's inputs. The sources are references, not authority to take external action. This pass is on demand; no live model execution is implied.

## Job
- story block or explicit skipped state
- eight-beat ledger
- verified details list
- at least two later callback anchor proposals
- handoff specification

## Inputs
- outline
- explanation_cards
- story_slot
- approved_story_facts_or_hypothetical_permission

## Allowed edits
- single story block
- story entrance proposal
- story handoff proposal
- callback anchors

## Protect
- Maximum one story
- Actual personal history
- Section teaching and explanation facts
- No magnet within story

## Owned rules
- R18: At most one main story, optional. Unannounced, connective start after grounding signpost, pain before scene, thought behind beats, mechanism withheld until handoff and later callbacks.
- R28: Story goes either after first segment has set lesson up, or later to overturn a belief blocking action. Later setup names belief and useful outcome. Never announce a story.
- R29: Story has eight ordered beats: pain before scene; wrong approach and thought; failure/bind; noticed cause leading to turn; action with expanded doubt; result and why feared outcome did not happen; missing practical piece; Parker handoff to teaching. Let beats breathe, not a form with one line each.
- R30: After story include at least two callbacks in different shapes and treat it as running example. No magnet mention inside story; move first mention after story if necessary.
- E01: Use movement archive as selective craft evidence. Retain its section payoff, grounding, simple teaching, varied questions, proof and callbacks; never import author biography, sales figures, tool claims or distinctive source wording as Josh facts.
