## Owned rules
- R15: Hook has opener, quick authority, then I will show you one to three things; at least one Parker line; about 50–70 words. Scene/build-up belongs in first section.
- R23: Parker line names a tangible counted item where count is true; outcome in viewer world; optional spoken even if/even when/without clause. Do not reveal mechanism or unexplained lesson names in the promise.
- R24: At least one Parker line in hook and one at every mechanics turn, usually two to four total. Never adjacent Parker lines or repeated wording; second promise of same thing is more specific.
- R25: Use relevant bracket-bank objections; omit forced ones. Avoid large homework-like counts and mechanisms posed as prizes.
- R26: Hook opener is curiosity, audience pain question or bold statement, no preamble/have you ever wondered. Authority must be true: supplied scale is more than 80,000 professionals and over 9,500 five-star reviews. Other claims need confirmation; lived pain alone does not establish expertise.
- R27: Hook promises fit title/thumbnail, vary shape, are each speakable in one breath and describe viewer payoff rather than watching the production. Aim under 25 seconds as well as about 50–70 words.
- P01: Use tangible curiosity plus outcome plus relevant objection. Do not import Parker hype, checkout layouts, proof, urgency or prices into scripts. Source itself says genuine urgency only.
- C01: Curiosity bank: broad pain, tangible item, true count, concrete outcome, relevant objection, no hype or unexplained terms. Four-to-eight page bullets is sales-page scope, not mandatory video count. Masterclass has no swipe file per this source; verify product identity.
