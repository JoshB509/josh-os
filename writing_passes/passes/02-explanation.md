## Owned rules
- R03: Skeleton includes hook in three beats, one or two sentences per section idea, one example/scene, close, optional story location, three confirmed magnet slots and all idea-to-mechanics turns. If idea needs more than a paragraph, simplify and let example carry it.
- R07: Every paragraph picks up from the previous paragraph with a spoken link. Introduce a thing before naming it, then define it, then show it.
- R11: Every pain and result is a concrete event, spoken response or inbox occurrence in the audience world. Replace vague outcomes using Part C.
- R22: Neutral explanatory/result lines are rewritten toward audience pain or result, rather than saying merely that something is effective.
- R33: Explain audience situation first, concept second and briefly. State formula once then phrases; do not repeat formula or identical objection handling for each phrase.
- A01: Write to common frustration rather than verbatim identifiable client story; moments rather than mechanisms in promises; preserve both audience profiles and their objections.
