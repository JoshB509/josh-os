## Owned rules
- R01: Rewrite a whole lesson from its brief with a fresh skeleton and fresh examples. Edit old lines only for a requested touch-up.
- R02: Before any whole-script writing or rewriting, ask whether a lead magnet is promoted unless already answered. If yes, require its name, viewer function and contents; never infer contents from its name. A paragraph/section touch-up does not trigger this question.
- R03: Skeleton includes hook in three beats, one or two sentences per section idea, one example/scene, close, optional story location, three confirmed magnet slots and all idea-to-mechanics turns. If idea needs more than a paragraph, simplify and let example carry it.
- R06: Keep one running example, image or number and call back to it across the script. When there is a story it usually supplies that example. Open loops must be genuine and paid off by giving the thing.
- R28: Story goes either after first segment has set lesson up, or later to overturn a belief blocking action. Later setup names belief and useful outcome. Never announce a story.
- R36: Select audience bank Part A first; deeper sources only as needed; selected source references do not grant authority to invent content.
- R49: Audience is capable professionals lacking recognition, decisions or progression; blend senior and less-senior scenes without splitting into two addressed groups.
- R50: Use all 24 frustration/result families A1–A14/K1–K10 as selectable banks, all B1–B31 as selectable movement forms, and Part C as prohibited-vagueness rewrite guidance.
- A01: Write to common frustration rather than verbatim identifiable client story; moments rather than mechanisms in promises; preserve both audience profiles and their objections.
- M01: Magnet index is an inventory of draft choices only. Ten rows conflict with stated total nine and built-asset summary. Require chosen magnet contents and availability.
