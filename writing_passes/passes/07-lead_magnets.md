## Owned rules
- R02: Before any whole-script writing or rewriting, ask whether a lead magnet is promoted unless already answered. If yes, require its name, viewer function and contents; never infer contents from its name. A paragraph/section touch-up does not trigger this question.
- R16: No download, subscribe or click CTA straight after hook; first real value comes first.
- R20: A selected confirmed magnet is mentioned three times after initial value, around middle and around 80 percent. Sell result rather than contents, each with clear free-description CTA.
- R30: After story include at least two callbacks in different shapes and treat it as running example. No magnet mention inside story; move first mention after story if necessary.
- R31: First magnet mention usually 25–35 percent after first value: shortest, problem/challenge → solution → CTA. Second around 50 percent after core reveal: doing it yourself is difficult, magnet helps. Third around 80 percent after main lesson: result without building from scratch. Each varied CTA says free in description/download now; never list features.
- R32: Client signals spread out, never in back-to-back sections or same paragraph as magnet CTA. They are swapped audience/client signposts or client value promises. No programme, price, booking call or offer link. Specific results require testimonials or confirmation; otherwise marked placeholder. Private client calls are unnamed and unrecognisable.
- M01: Magnet index is an inventory of draft choices only. Ten rows conflict with stated total nine and built-asset summary. Require chosen magnet contents and availability.
- P01: Use tangible curiosity plus outcome plus relevant objection. Do not import Parker hype, checkout layouts, proof, urgency or prices into scripts. Source itself says genuine urgency only.
- C01: Curiosity bank: broad pain, tangible item, true count, concrete outcome, relevant objection, no hype or unexplained terms. Four-to-eight page bullets is sales-page scope, not mandatory video count. Masterclass has no swipe file per this source; verify product identity.
