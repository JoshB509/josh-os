## Owned rules
- R04: Each section has a signpost, grounding line, short idea, movement and closing line/handoff. Use B1–B12 or Parker for entrance, B13 grounding, B14/B3 close.
- R08: Match signposts to lesson position. Early entrances set up and announce; warnings, rankings and step labels follow established parts. Judge with the paragraphs before and after.
- R13: Signpost pull is pain, result, stakes or question in the line or next line. So you can / so you do not occurs in no more than one signpost in three and never adjacent signposts.
- R14: Do not make signposts about the mechanism, presenter, meta diagnosis, insight/understanding as prize or something the viewer must decode.
- R17: Every idea-to-tangible-mechanics turn uses a Parker line: tangible thing, result and relevant objection. No forcing an objection when none fits.
- R19: Use two or three subtle client signals per full script; no other offer detail.
- R23: Parker line names a tangible counted item where count is true; outcome in viewer world; optional spoken even if/even when/without clause. Do not reveal mechanism or unexplained lesson names in the promise.
- R24: At least one Parker line in hook and one at every mechanics turn, usually two to four total. Never adjacent Parker lines or repeated wording; second promise of same thing is more specific.
- R25: Use relevant bracket-bank objections; omit forced ones. Avoid large homework-like counts and mechanisms posed as prizes.
- R32: Client signals spread out, never in back-to-back sections or same paragraph as magnet CTA. They are swapped audience/client signposts or client value promises. No programme, price, booking call or offer link. Specific results require testimonials or confirmation; otherwise marked placeholder. Private client calls are unnamed and unrecognisable.
- R50: Use all 24 frustration/result families A1–A14/K1–K10 as selectable banks, all B1–B31 as selectable movement forms, and Part C as prohibited-vagueness rewrite guidance.
- P01: Use tangible curiosity plus outcome plus relevant objection. Do not import Parker hype, checkout layouts, proof, urgency or prices into scripts. Source itself says genuine urgency only.
- C01: Curiosity bank: broad pain, tangible item, true count, concrete outcome, relevant objection, no hype or unexplained terms. Four-to-eight page bullets is sales-page scope, not mandatory video count. Masterclass has no swipe file per this source; verify product identity.
- E01: Use movement archive as selective craft evidence. Retain its section payoff, grounding, simple teaching, varied questions, proof and callbacks; never import author biography, sales figures, tool claims or distinctive source wording as Josh facts.
