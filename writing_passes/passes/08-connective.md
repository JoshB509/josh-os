## Owned rules
- R05: Inside sections use a movement device every two to four sentences, never the same device twice in a row. Mix B15–B30. Maximum four sentences without a move is retained; do not silently downgrade this quota.
- R06: Keep one running example, image or number and call back to it across the script. When there is a story it usually supplies that example. Open loops must be genuine and paid off by giving the thing.
- R07: Every paragraph picks up from the previous paragraph with a spoken link. Introduce a thing before naming it, then define it, then show it.
- R08: Match signposts to lesson position. Early entrances set up and announce; warnings, rankings and step labels follow established parts. Judge with the paragraphs before and after.
- R09: Say concrete events that cause feelings; never announce the feeling with labels such as here is the bit that stings.
- R10: Do not use I said I would show you, remember I said, because I said or equivalent announcements of a routine payoff. Give the payoff directly.
- R11: Every pain and result is a concrete event, spoken response or inbox occurrence in the audience world. Replace vague outcomes using Part C.
- R12: Do not repeat the same pain/result wording in a lesson. Rotate the relevant A/K family. Callbacks retain the referent while varying wording.
- R30: After story include at least two callbacks in different shapes and treat it as running example. No magnet mention inside story; move first mention after story if necessary.
- R50: Use all 24 frustration/result families A1–A14/K1–K10 as selectable banks, all B1–B31 as selectable movement forms, and Part C as prohibited-vagueness rewrite guidance.
- E01: Use movement archive as selective craft evidence. Retain its section payoff, grounding, simple teaching, varied questions, proof and callbacks; never import author biography, sales figures, tool claims or distinctive source wording as Josh facts.
