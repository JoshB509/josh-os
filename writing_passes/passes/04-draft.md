## Owned rules
- R01: Rewrite a whole lesson from its brief with a fresh skeleton and fresh examples. Edit old lines only for a requested touch-up.
- R04: Each section has a signpost, grounding line, short idea, movement and closing line/handoff. Use B1–B12 or Parker for entrance, B13 grounding, B14/B3 close.
- R05: Inside sections use a movement device every two to four sentences, never the same device twice in a row. Mix B15–B30. Maximum four sentences without a move is retained; do not silently downgrade this quota.
- R06: Keep one running example, image or number and call back to it across the script. When there is a story it usually supplies that example. Open loops must be genuine and paid off by giving the thing.
- R16: No download, subscribe or click CTA straight after hook; first real value comes first.
- R18: At most one main story, optional. Unannounced, connective start after grounding signpost, pain before scene, thought behind beats, mechanism withheld until handoff and later callbacks.
- R21: Close follows core lesson without summary or wrap-up, opens next genuine curiosity gap and sends to next video/lesson.
- R22: Neutral explanatory/result lines are rewritten toward audience pain or result, rather than saying merely that something is effective.
- R30: After story include at least two callbacks in different shapes and treat it as running example. No magnet mention inside story; move first mention after story if necessary.
- R33: Explain audience situation first, concept second and briefly. State formula once then phrases; do not repeat formula or identical objection handling for each phrase.
- R34: Close is at most four short beats: new gap, one/two lines on it, needed thing and value-bearing next-video send. Next resource addresses real prerequisite or new problem; viewer still received whole promised lesson.
- R35: Close forbids ending/recap phrases: so that is the whole thing, to wrap up, before you go, that is it for today, thanks for watching, I hope this helped, that is the next video, there is one thing I did not tell you, all of that works inside the conversation but; no covered-topics list.
- R49: Audience is capable professionals lacking recognition, decisions or progression; blend senior and less-senior scenes without splitting into two addressed groups.
- R50: Use all 24 frustration/result families A1–A14/K1–K10 as selectable banks, all B1–B31 as selectable movement forms, and Part C as prohibited-vagueness rewrite guidance.
- A01: Write to common frustration rather than verbatim identifiable client story; moments rather than mechanisms in promises; preserve both audience profiles and their objections.
- E01: Use movement archive as selective craft evidence. Retain its section payoff, grounding, simple teaching, varied questions, proof and callbacks; never import author biography, sales figures, tool claims or distinctive source wording as Josh facts.
