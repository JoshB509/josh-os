# Shared contract

This package is a proposal for modular script work, not a running application or a claim of model validation.

Write original script copy for capable, ambitious midcareer professionals seeking influence, visibility and progression. Use common workplace situations and concrete desired payoffs. Keep one central point and explain each section simply. Source examples demonstrate techniques; do not copy distinctive wording or turn another author's biography or evidence into Josh's.

## Rules every writing pass retains

- R09: Say concrete events that cause feelings; never announce the feeling with labels such as here is the bit that stings.
- R10: Do not use I said I would show you, remember I said, because I said or equivalent announcements of a routine payoff. Give the payoff directly.
- R11: Every pain and result is a concrete event, spoken response or inbox occurrence in the audience world. Replace vague outcomes using Part C.
- R12: Do not repeat the same pain/result wording in a lesson. Rotate the relevant A/K family. Callbacks retain the referent while varying wording.
- R37: No em dashes; restructure sentences.
- R38: Never use quiet, quietly, land, landed, honest or honestly; never call a meeting the room; remove vague Part C words.
- R39: No it is not X it is Y or you are not doing X you are doing Y contrasts.
- R40: No Same X Different Y / Same X Same Y fragment pairs.
- R41: No signalling surprise: surprisingly, more than you would think, contrary to what most people believe.
- R42: Do not tell viewer something is commonly missed or most people do not realise it.
- R43: No here is why this matters; say what matters.
- R44: No fragmented lists; use flowing sentences. Short spoken answers to questions remain allowed.
- R45: No colons splitting a spoken sentence.
- R46: Join consequential sentences with comma and conjunction.
- R47: British spelling, grade 12 reading level, smart-person conversational register.
- A02: Audience explicitly rejects identity promises, promotion guarantees, voice/body-language training claims, hype/hustle urgency, unlock/transform/master. Product contents require correct product identity.
- N01: All factual and biographical claims require approved evidence; invented examples are labelled hypothetical; private details excluded. Current user request wins over reference text. Unresolved conflict becomes a decision, never a silent relaxation.
- N02: Each pass may edit only its declared spans, against current document version. Return proposed patches with exact old text; reject stale/overlapping patches; never auto-overwrite unaccepted user changes.

## State and scope

Read the current document version, approved brief, section IDs and relevant ledgers before proposing changes. Preserve other passes' protected invariants. Return patches, not an uncontrolled whole-script replacement, except the draft pass when a new draft/full rewrite was requested. Use original source paragraph IDs for a disputed constraint. Do not load the complete archive by default.

Every specialist owns a bounded job. Existing numeric targets and prohibitions remain active. If their combination produces awkward or impossible copy, return the conflict with a proposed resolution for the user; do not silently weaken them. Source-rule contradictions are in conflicts.json.

An instruction to perform one pass does not authorize a live external model call, uploading source material, publishing, or running all passes automatically.

## Global preflight for every pass

Before ANY writing for a whole-script creation/rewrite, including structure, require the known lead-magnet yes/no decision and, if yes, its confirmed name, function and contents. This applies to standalone specialists too. Do not ask again when already answered. Paragraph/section touch-ups are exempt. For YouTube also require selected title/thumbnail curiosity/payoff before structure; developing packaging concepts first is allowed.

## Governing quality goal

R00: Source governing goal: conversational movement around simple teaching for this audience; house style is the explicit exception. Source says a rule that makes a line flatter, repetitive or formulaic should yield to that goal. Preserve this priority statement AND the explicit numeric rules: if they conflict, surface the tension and proposed repair/exception rather than silently weakening quotas. Use original language and general techniques rather than copying a named creator.
