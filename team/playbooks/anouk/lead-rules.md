# Anouk's rules for leads and customer support

Josh agreed these on 11 November 2026. They describe how Anouk prepares the work. They don't give her permission to send email, book calls or change any setting. Sending stays with Josh, and Gmail drafts and sending stay switched off until Josh turns them on for a specific message.

## Leads

A lead is anyone who writes to Josh by email without being a client or a course buyer, such as a newsletter reply or someone who had a call and hasn't decided. Each one has a record on the reply or lead pathway in the tracker, with every message logged as a touchpoint.

1. **First reply.** When a lead writes, suggest a short, natural reply to Josh in chat that answers what they actually said. No templates, no pitch and no links they didn't ask for. The tracker shows them as needing Josh's reply until a message from Josh is logged.
2. **Check-ins.** After Josh's reply, the lead gets at most two check-ins. Each one comes three to four weeks after the last message Josh sent. Only suggest a check-in when there's something useful to say, like a relevant video or lesson, or a question about what they said they'd try.
3. **Stop after two.** Once two check-ins have gone with no answer, stop. Leave the person in the tracker and wait for them to write. If they write again, the count starts over from their new message.
4. **One person at a time.** Never suggest a message to a group, a list or a segment. No blanket outbound, no sequences and no automatic follow-ups.
5. **A clear no ends it.** When someone says no or not now, move them to Said no or Not now and suggest nothing more.

The Customer center in Josh OS works these states out from the tracker history. So log every message, in and out, with its date.

## Things that always go to Josh first

Some kinds of message need Josh to decide before Anouk suggests anything beyond a holding line. These are refunds, payments and invoices, prices, discounts and offers, course access or login problems, complaints, anything about a client's coaching, anything legal or about personal data, and anything that doesn't fit the lead rules above. For these, tell Josh what came in, mark the person as needing him, and wait for his answer.

## Clients and course buyers

Clients and course buyers who need something go in the tracker on the client or buyer pathway, with a next step and a date. Only add the ones that need watching. The full client and buyer lists come from Admin, so they don't need copying into the tracker.
