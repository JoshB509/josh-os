DaJoshiResolve - reusable editorial rules
16 October 2026; requirements to implement and verify, not claims of capability.
Authority: APPROVED_EDITING_WORKFLOW.txt and Josh's
Sentinel_a11fc6bb26ac819197fe7b56c96d91a8 feedback.

R01. Separate Pass 1, Pass 1 approval, Pass 2 and clean-cut approval. Never infer
     a new pass approval from an older fixture or successful job exit code.
R02. Supplied clean mic is the intended speech source. Measure sync against the
     actual video, verify drift and protect source bounds. Keying/background/
     shadow/subtle moving light change appearance, never the source clock.
R03. Accurate words need evidence: retain source coordinates, confidence and
     validation. A transcript alone is not proof of a complete or clean cut.
R04. Cut silence for natural pacing. Protect initial consonants, endings,
     breaths and intentional emphasis. Resolve joins acoustically and by listening.
R05. Tiny audio crossfades are conditional. Choose safe handles, duration and
     curve per join; avoid doubled words, phase artifacts and rejected speech.
     Keep video joins straight unless a specific visual transition is designed.
     Do not shorten output duration through an implicit acrossfade operation.
R06. Sweep the tightened transcript AND audio semantically. Remove incomplete
     rephrased starts even when token overlap is low. Preserve deliberate repeats
     and valid improvisation. Record uncertain cases instead of guessing.
R07. A rejected range must disappear from retained spans, actual audio/video and
     exported XML. Verify absence; recording a decision/flag is insufficient.
R08. Rebuild the map after removals. Remap only words safely inside kept spans;
     retranscribe/re-align affected words/joins or the full cut when necessary.
     Preserve provenance; fail unresolved bounds, ordering and coverage errors.
R09. Approved kept timeline is the single authority. All shots and cues bind to
     its exact revision. Changes to its timing require a new reviewable revision;
     visuals cannot silently re-time it. Import manual approved cuts faithfully.
R10. Every graphic/PiP shot declares entry and exit. Once its purpose/read time
     ends, return to presenter unless another explicit shot follows. Long waits
     for the next animation beat are not a reason to remain on a graphic.
R11. Multi-part videos use recurring progressive checkpoints at transitions:
     completed/current/upcoming states, consistent labels and visual layout.
     Render the correct cumulative state directly, without replaying old reveals.
R12. Isolate scenes, settle incoming state and preserve intended animation phase
     across splits. Motion duration is explicit; three seconds is not universal.
R13. Keep editable graphics, reversible presenter layouts, originals and recovery
     handles. Handle availability does not authorize indefinite graphic holds.
R14. Test data integrity, native playback, listening and editorial approval
     separately. A valid XML or passing automated test is not editorial signoff.
R15. Work in new isolated runs with backups. Never overwrite production manual
     cuts, disturb active Premiere/OBS, or publish as part of local planning.
